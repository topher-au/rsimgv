title: Navigation, tabs and bookmarks desc: The path bar, location entry, history, tabs, bookmarks, properties and mouse buttons in rsimgv. ---

There are several ways to get where you are going: breadcrumbs, a typed path, history, the folder tree, tabs, bookmarks, and folder-to-folder stepping that never leaves the keyboard.

The path bar

The header holds a path bar of clickable breadcrumbs. It starts at your home folder when you are inside it, shown as Home, and at / otherwise. Long names shrink in the middle so the whole path stays in view.

  • Click a segment to open that folder here.
  • Middle-click a segment to open it in a background tab.
  • Ctrl-click a segment to open it in a new window.
  • Right-click a segment for its context menu.
  • Hover a segment while dragging files and it opens after a short delay.

Typing a location

{{shot location-bar "The path bar turned into a text entry"}}

Click the current folder in the path bar, or press Ctrl+L, and it becomes a text entry. Type a path and press Enter. A leading ~ means your home folder. Ctrl+O opens a folder chooser instead.

History

The header has back, forward and up buttons. They also answer to Alt+←, Alt+→ and Alt+↑. Long-press back or forward to list the history in that direction and jump straight to an entry. Up stops at the root.

The mouse's side buttons are bound to back and forward by default, and can be changed to up, previous item, next item or nothing in Controls → Mouse.

Stepping between folders

Four actions walk the folder tree without touching it. None has a default key, so bind the ones you want in the keybinding editor:

ActionWhat it does
win.next-folder, win.previous-folderGo to the next or previous folder in depth-first order of the folder tree. In the viewer, go to the next or previous folder with a picture or video and show its first one.
win.next-media-folder, win.previous-media-folderGo to the next or previous folder that contains a picture or video, selecting its first item (or its last, going backwards).

In the viewer, When reaching the end (viewer.folder-end) decides what stepping past the first or last item does: stop, loop around the folder, or go on to the next-folder that has media.

Tabs

Each tab is a window onto a folder and keeps its own state: its folder, history, tree selection, scroll position and selected item. Open one by middle-clicking a folder in the tree, the grid or the path bar, or by passing several arguments on the command line. The tab bar runs along the bottom of the content area and appears when more than one tab is open. Right-click a tab for its menu:

  • Duplicate opens another tab on the same folder.
  • New window moves the folder to a window of its own.
  • Copy path puts the folder's path on the clipboard.
  • Close others and Close.

Tabs can also be listed, opened, activated and closed through the remote control interface.

Bookmarks

{{shot bookmark-dialog "Bookmarking the current folder"}}

Press Ctrl+D to bookmark the folder you are in. A dialog asks for a name, which defaults to the folder's own; bookmarking a folder that is already bookmarked renames it. Bookmarks collect in a collapsible list above the folder tree, and win.toggle-bookmarks expands or collapses it.

{{shot bookmarks-sidebar "A bookmark in the sidebar, above the folder tree"}}

Drag a bookmark to reorder it, middle-click it to open a new tab, or right-click it to rename or remove it. The bookmark of the folder shown is highlighted. Bookmarks are kept in bookmarks.yaml beside the settings file, and can be added, listed and removed remotely with bookmarks.add, bookmarks.list and bookmarks.remove.

Properties

Right-click an item or a bookmark and choose Properties for its name, type, location, size, modification time and, for a picture or video, its resolution, duration and codecs where they are known.

Start location

A new window opens on the folder you last had open, at /, at your home folder, or at a specific folder, as Start location says. When it is the last folder, the item you last had selected is selected again.
