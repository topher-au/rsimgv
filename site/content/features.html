Features

A tour of rsimgv with screenshots. Each section links to the documentation that covers it in full. Click any screenshot to enlarge it.

Browse and search

Folder tree and browse grid

The sidebar holds a folder tree rooted at / or your home folder. Folders expand on demand, and selecting one reveals its path. The grid shows the current folder: folders first, then files, in natural name order, so img2 comes before img10.

Only pictures and videos are listed by default, and the header's buttons choose whether pictures, videos or both appear. Turn the media filter off to see every file.

Search a folder or everything under it

Ctrl+Shift+F slides a search bar down under the header. Filter by name, by any attribute, or by size, modified time or type. Turn on Subfolders to search the whole tree under the folder shown, with the matches listed together.

{{shot browse-search "Searching a folder from the header"}}

Folder previews

With folder previews on, each folder icon carries a cascade of its first pictures and videos. Choose how many, let them fit the pictures' proportions, or cycle through the folder's media while the pointer is over it.

{{shot folder-previews "Folders showing a cascade of the media inside them"}}

Videos preview themselves

Video thumbnails come from a frame a tenth of the way in. While a video is the active item, its preview can play a sequence of frames spread through the video, or an embedded player in the item's frame. Frames are made with ffmpeg and cached.

{{shot browse-videos "Video thumbnails in the browse grid"}}

Navigate

Path bar and manual entry

The header's path bar has a clickable breadcrumb for each folder. It starts at home when you are inside it, and at / otherwise. Click the current folder, or press Ctrl+L, to type a path; a leading ~ means your home folder.

Back, forward and up buttons sit beside it. Long-press back or forward to list the history in that direction. Tabs run along the bottom of the content area.

Bookmarks

Press Ctrl+D to bookmark the folder you are in, under a name you choose. Bookmarks collect in a collapsible list above the folder tree, and can be dragged into a new order.

Middle-click a folder to open it in a background tab; Ctrl-click a path segment for a new window.

{{shot bookmarks-sidebar "A bookmark in the sidebar above the folder tree"}}

View pictures

Fit, zoom and pan

Pictures open fitted to the window, to its width or height, filled to cover it, or at their own size. Zoom in steps you choose, pan with the arrow keys, and focus one-to-one at the centre, the top or the point under the pointer.

Scaling can be nearest-neighbour, bilinear or bicubic, optionally sharpened. The bicubic and sharpening paths are computed by OpenCV.

Rotate, mirror and toggle the viewer

Turn a picture or video a quarter turn either way, mirror it, or set a rotation from a key. B toggles the viewer and the grid without changing what is shown, so you can look at either without losing your place.

{{shot viewer-image "The viewer with a pinned thumbnail bar and the status bar"}}

Thumbnail bar, status bar and overlay

The thumbnail bar can sit on any edge, float over the picture or sit beside it, and stay put or hide until the pointer comes near. The status bar shows the item's position, name, resolution, frame rate and file size, and the configurable overlay draws the seek, volume, speed and status displays.

Play video

libmpv playback

Videos play through libmpv by default, rendered in software into the viewer or with OpenGL, or through GStreamer by way of GTK's own media backend. Position, speed, volume and aspect ratio can be remembered per file, found by the file's contents so they follow a file that is moved or renamed.

Skipping through a video shows a progress bar and the time over it. Its place, bar width, time format and visibility are settings, and the video controls can be dragged and resized when unlocked.

Crop, aspect ratio and backdrop

Strip black bars or a blurred backdrop from a video by detecting the picture, or drag the crop yourself. Keep the detected shape or choose 4:3, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 3:2, 2:3 or 21:9, set the output aspect ratio, and fill the space round the picture with a blurred copy of it.

{{shot video-crop "Editing a video's crop"}}

Fullscreen

Press F11 for fullscreen, and pin the viewer header and the video controls with P. The cursor hides itself while the viewer is idle.

{{shot video-fullscreen "A video in fullscreen"}}

SVP frame interpolation

Videos played through libmpv can be interpolated by SmoothVideo Project. The open-svpflow plugins are built and embedded, so SVP itself need not be installed, and the output rate can follow the source, a fixed target, the target divided by the playback speed, or the display's rate divided by the speed.

Playlist and history

A playlist from anywhere

Collect pictures and videos from any folder into one list, reorder it, shuffle it, and save or load it as M3U, PLS or XSPF. The panel sits opposite the sidebar, can be detached into its own window, and keeps the made order, the shuffled order, what has been played and where you left off.

The playlist →

{{shot playlist-panel "The playlist panel beside the browse view"}}

Viewing history

rsimgv counts how many times each picture or video has been viewed, and how long it has been watched, measured by an in-app timer. The totals show in the viewer and the browse grid, and Viewing History lists recent files. A view can need a few seconds, a share of a video, or both, and you choose.

Customise

Keybindings in seven contexts

The same key can do different things depending on where you are: browsing, in the viewer, looking at a picture, watching a video, in the playlist, or in the folder tree. Rebind anything in the editor, which also searches by action or shortcut. Press F1 for a list of what is bound where you are.

Keybindings and mouse →

{{shot keybinds-overlay-viewer "The F1 overlay listing the keys in force in the viewer"}}

A keybinding editor

Every action, its description and its keys in one searchable list, with a context for each binding. Bind a key to run a script, to show a notification, to seek a video by an exact amount or a number of frames, or to set a volume target or aspect ratio.

{{shot prefs-keybindings "The Keyboard page of the preferences"}}

Mouse and scroll wheel

Choose what the back and forward buttons do, and what the wheel does over a picture and over a video.

{{shot prefs-mouse "Mouse and scrolling preferences"}}

Settings that apply at once

Preferences are stored as YAML in ~/.config/rsimgv/rsimgv.conf and applied the moment they change, whether you edit them in the dialog or in the file.

Automate

Scripts

List commands on the Scripts page, with %file% standing for the item they run on. Bind a key to one and it runs on the item under the cursor, or the one the viewer shows. A script that waits to finish has the folder listed again when it ends, and a script that fails says so with a message.

Plugins

Python plugins add actions, keybindings, menus, events, sidebar panels and their own settings. They reload when their files change, and the bundled LLM Screenshot plugin sends the viewer's picture or frame to an OpenAI-compatible vision model.

Plugins →

{{shot prefs-plugins "The Plugins page under Integration"}}

Remote control

A JSON interface served over a Unix socket, TCP or HTTP. Run any action, open tabs, select files, read the current item, take a screenshot, read file metadata and thumbnails, get and set file state, change settings, drive plugins, and show messages over the window.
